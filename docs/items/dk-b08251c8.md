# Split containment.mjs and registered-checkout.mjs when they next grow

Found in the 0.8.0 push review (componentization lens). Watch item, no action now.

- `src/repository/containment.mjs` holds lexical path validation (`relativeInside`, `lexicallyWithin`) and the filesystem checkout guard (`assertSafeCheckout`, `isLink`, `assertRealWithin`). Consumers and reasons to change differ. On a third containment rule, move the guard to `checkout-guard.mjs`.
- `src/repository/registered-checkout.mjs` mixes the stateless `openCheckoutAt` with the TTL-cached `createCheckoutOpener`, which depends on registry shapes. After dk-d041ec4b, consider moving `openCheckoutAt` next to `context.mjs`.
